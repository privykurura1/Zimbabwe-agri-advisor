**Request for technical review and a pilot of Murimi Fara, an offline farm advisor**

Dear [Name],

I am writing to ask for your office's help in checking and piloting Murimi Fara, a
farm advice program that runs on an ordinary laptop with no internet. Farmers
describe a sick animal or damaged crop in English, Shona or Ndebele. The program
answers only from a written knowledge base of [55] entries on cattle, goat and
poultry diseases and maize, groundnut and tomato problems, and it sends urgent cases
to a vet or extension officer. A photo check for fall armyworm on maize is included.

What we are asking for:
1. **Technical review:** a vet and/or agronomist checks each knowledge entry using
   the review sheet we provide (about [X] hours of work). Nothing goes to farmers
   until it is signed off.
2. **A small pilot:** [8] weeks with [30–60] farmers in [ward/district]. An officer
   reads a sample of answers each week, and our outbreak-watch page shows unusual
   rises in questions about a disease.
3. **Correct contacts:** the vet, AGRITEX and dip-tank contacts farmers should be
   referred to in the area.

What we provide: the software and installation, training for officers, the weekly
data summaries (counts only, no farmer names), and fixes to any advice found to be
wrong. The pilot plan is attached.

The program does not replace extension officers or vets. It is designed to get
farmers to them sooner, with a clearer description of the problem.

I would be grateful for a short meeting at your convenience.

Yours sincerely,
